# Places and positions from the campaign documents

From the author's Google Docs (Drachenhorn arc; "3121 AA"; chapters 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15). Only places, landmarks and where they are.
"Atlas" says what the map has now. Positions are in world px (x, y) where the atlas already has the place.

## Berhagen / Hagensport (3121 AA) — Selladorian Free Nations, south of the Borderwode
- **Hagensport** — port town, new, growing, prosperous on sea trade; baronies of the heroes. Main crossroads has the Wandering Wind tavern. Atlas: port (95, 286). A trade road runs **west along the coast** through rough country to **Allstone** in Gideon (Atlas: Allstone (41, 272)).
- **New Sellador** — Sellador's name for **Berhagen** (author, 2026-11-01): some years ago, in a time of unrest, Sellador claimed this land from the Free Nations and treats it as land to be settled; "along the seashore south of the Borderwode" (ch. 15 land grants). It is a different place from **New Sellador Port** (Curmeah, 549, 702), which stays where it is. Atlas: region label "New Sellador" (84, 286) restored, note added to Berhagen.
- **Lenarte farmstead** — a few days **north and west** of Hagensport; the Border Mountains show far in the west on clear days. Not on the atlas.
- **Selladorian garrison / regiment** left **north and east** toward the lands bordering the **Crow Fells** (Atlas: Crow Fells (111, 283)).
- **Alderford** — logging (and mining) town, **north and west** of the gnoll lands, at a crossing of the river **Thalweg Wyne**; mayor/foreman Samantha Dunstan. Atlas: Alderford (91, 283). River name not on the atlas.
- **Hearthden** — Silvermane gnoll village in the deep woods (the party is exiled from it). Not on the atlas.
- **Goldfang village** and **Caer Carithal** (druid cave) — Goldfang gnoll tribe. Atlas: Goldfang Village (121, 263), Caer Carithal (123, 269).
- **Caer Yar-Ahn-Og (Bone Fields)** — ancient cave of the Silvermane druids, burial place, portal to Faerie; inside: Bone Fields, Cistern Cave, the Anastomosing tunnels, lush caverns. Not on the atlas; in the gnoll woods between Hagensport and Alderford.
- **Ierru and the Crow Fells** — Atlas: Ierru (105, 284), Crow Fells (111, 283).
- **Rheulgrad** — "destroyed village in the woods", destroyed 3057 AA (64 years before 3121), haunted tavern (ghosts, can't leave), well fed by a sacred spring in the mountains. Author: Rheulgrad is **ruins**. Atlas: now kind ruin at (102, 279), with a ruined street plan.
- Ruined stone cabin at the woods' edge (the Archer family home). Kopuri (Feywild village, Feywild castle), Highriver (see Drachenhorn).

## Drachenhorn (Drachenhorn arc doc)
- A large city in a **broad pass between Sellador (west) and the Free Nations (east)**; nearly all overland trade between them passes through; mountains on both sides impassable. Atlas: city (93, 270).
- Author: the city is **older than the Selladorian occupation**; when Sellador claimed lands to its east it took over Drachenhorn and installed its own governor (about 50 years ago, a free city before). The old database entry and the item "Elana's Ward" (ch. 15) also mention 2945 AA, when the dragon Tzelgoriath was killed (Elana Firemantle, Horus, Edom Skaelan), and the founding of the city after it.
- The valley floor **tilts**: west approach about 40 ft higher than the east. Mountain walls rise sharply on the **north and south**; the oldest houses are cut into the rock. The **Highriver enters from the north-west**, water and the whole sewer system run **east** to drainage exits near the **eastern gate**.
- **Trade Road** runs west to east through the city (about twenty minutes' walk). **Marktplatz** (market square, tiered Gothic fountain fed by the Highriver) at its midpoint. **Grand Crucible** (arena) forms the **east edge of the Marktplatz**. **Governor's Residence** ("the Rathaus") at the **west end** of the Trade Road, just inside the gate. **The Glass Spider** (inn): on a narrow street running **north** off the Trade Road, **three blocks west of the Marktplatz**. **The Last Round** (tavern) and the **Charnel Market** (auction warehouse): near the Crucible, by its service entrance.
- Districts: **Upper Quarter** on the north slope (oldest, rich; Church of the Eternal Sacred Light with a bell tower on its north end); **Work Quarter** on the south side (flatter, trades); **Low District / Niederwald** against the **east wall** (poorest, the Thieves Guild's base). Eastern sewers sealed by the watch.
- Style: grey-green and blue-grey alpine stone, heavy dark timber above, overhanging eaves (Swiss), Gothic gables and pointed arches, slate roofs. (This does not yet match the generated radial plan: the real Drachenhorn is a linear pass city.)

## Peregrine's Table (ch. 4) — island west of Sellador
- "One of the few islands on the maps of Sellador"; Mandiant's charts: a small jut of rock covered in sea-bird droppings. Atlas: Peregrine's Islet (-54, 284).
- **Eastern hill** (toward the sunrise): a cave, the lair of two dire boars, with a small hidden chamber of clay jars of old coin (Selladorian, elven, dwarven and **Atman**: "Empire... Atma") and a painting of islands with one above the others hurling lightning (Akanos).
- **Western hill**: a flat rock set on two others like a table: a **Sahuagin altar to Shokath**, the shark god.

## Lochlainn (ch. 6, 8)
- **Cuankarig** (Cuan Karig) — small port town, attacked by pirates; the party lands **just north of the town**. Atlas: Cuan Karig (85, 347).
- **Lochlainn** — original elven name **Arandir**, founded 1453 AA by the Arlantil elves.
- **Lonial Mountains** — natural border between **north and south Lochlainn**, former lands of Clan McKrae. Atlas: Lonial Mountains (101, 375).
- **Caer Ordaich-Uaine** — the fortress at the **centre of northern Lochlainn**, headquarters of the Green Order (75 years old), built in the ruin of the old fortress city **Casadh-Abhainn**; walls overgrown; "the heart of the northlands", south of Cuankarig. Includes the Crow's Eyes tavern and a library.
- **Aillfar** (Ailfar) — port city to the **north-west** of Caer Ordaich-Uaine; the Inferno is moored here.
- **Skellig Rath** — a bleak rocky island **about a day's sail from Aillfar**, lair of the formorian Elgol (protected by the Lonial mountains).
- **Castle McKrae / "the Devil's Ruin" / Daerunbrae** (ch. 7) — a ruined castle once on a mountain, half in the material plane and half in hell; dungeon under the town's streets (an empty well, sewers, crypt, reliquary, a portal to an infernal courtyard, a spiral stair to an **underground lake** that connects by water to the forests beyond the mountain, with a **sinkhole in the nearby forest**). Lair of the young green dragon Kozgoroth; the first key to Storm Tower is here.
- **Galiad** — small city in **central Lochlainn**, wiped out in 2978 AA in the Ruin. **Caer Fid-Nemith** — former druid grove, destroyed by the mountain of fire in 2986 AA, then home of the **Chaos Gate** (sealed 3027 AA). Atlas: Caer Fid-Nemith ruin (98, 358); the Chaos Gate entry is still unplaced.
- **The Ageanath** — former Garanlyon, now sea druids of the deep west ("Tel-Ageanath").

## The western islands / Akanos (ch. 9, 11, 12, 13, 14)
- **Akanos (Aramantha, Stormtower)** — the flying kingdom; its lords took it far out to sea, settled colonies on islands in the **western seas**; ruins of lost colonies lie west (the book of Aden Ainmire).
- **Prios** — one of the first colony islands. A **ridge across the middle** (Kapaneios's tower on the ridge), the **Ruins of Diakanon** (only columns and street patterns left; reached from the beach camp inland, towards a bluish light on the ridge), a burrow workshop at the end of a ruined street (Athanasia the medusa), **the Waters of Aranor** (a fountain in a forgotten shrine), a **portal** to the other Atman colonies. Atlas: Prios, Kapanêios Tower (-173, 301), Settlement Ruins (-174, 302).
- **Thanatos** — island of the vampires and Nethrak; the **Sun Chaser** ran aground; **Andros' Lab**; **Nethrak's keep** (Halls of the Honored, the Throne of Nethrak, a hidden passage down to a flooded cave and the Dragon's Tomb, the Vault of Nethrak); the Raavenites' home. Atlas: Thanatos, Andros' Lab (-237, 345), Wreckage of the Sun Chaser (-235, 346).
- **Agrea** — beneath it the sacred underworld **Arali** and below that the water realm **Enguru**; **Caer Khymer** (ancient Agrean temple where the druids gather); **Mount Akamu** (author: the Agrea volcano; the docs also write Veasna / Vaesna / Ishme) with the **Temple of Akamu** (ch. 14) and the **Vivarium** inside; a **cenote**; **Sunken Caves**. Atlas: Agrea, Caer Khimer (-299, 466), Sunken Caves (-299, 467), Mount Akamu (-300, 465).
- **The Collapse (Absym Collapse)** — an underwater place beneath a ruined city (the lost **Akanosi Library District**) on the sea floor, where the Embrian sleeps; the Aboleth Ubel'gorath's chamber, the Skum lair, the Sunken Library.
- **The Umbral Isle** (ch. 15) — a cave entrance flanked by bronze dragon statues, on an isolated island far to the **north**; "The Three" live in a cave there; a way to the plane of shadow.
